**Rescue NoClip — SPT 4.1.6**

Spawned outside Interchange or stuck somewhere you can’t escape? Rescue NoClip lets you fly through obstacles and return to a playable area.

**Controls**
- **F8:** Toggle NoClip.
- **WASD:** Move/Fly around.
- **Space / Left Ctrl:** Move up / down.
- **Left Shift:** Fly faster.
- Use the BepInEx menu (F12) Configure the toggle key and flight speed.

Before disabling NoClip, move just above clear, solid ground.

**Compatibility**
Lightweight and designed to minimize conflicts with other SPT 4.1.6 mods. Compatibility with many different mods currently available for Tushonka 4.1.6 has been tested by me, however I didn't go through every single one of them.

**Warning**
Intended only for recovering from out-of-bounds spawns or getting unstuck. Disable it once you reach a safe location. NoClip does not protect against kill zones or other damage.
